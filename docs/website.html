Friends and Smiles... quick about us!

Welcome to my website! This is my passion project and little creative space. Come on in and welcome to my home! [This is a WIP and will forever be a WIP]

Friends and Smiles came from the song "Where is smiley?" by Serani Poji. I remember scrolling somewhere with muy bestfriend and coming across one of the sites like neocities where people had posted their own websites, and one random day in september I decided that this was my calling! I really like the decora style, with my room being decorated with rainbows wall paintings, beads, and small crafts I stuck on the wall so what better than to make it styled like that! Right now it may be a bit basic and not rainbow but trust it is coming! Thank you for visiting my website!
